How to play

Goal
Each stage has an enemy with HP. Clearing lines deals damage. Kill it before it kills you. Survive all 5 stages to win. Death = run over (roguelike!).
Damage
Single 1 · Double 3 · Triple 5 · Tetris 8 · +combo bonus · Back-to-back Tetris ×1.5. Perfect clear = +6.
Enemy attacks
When the red timer fills, the enemy strikes: garbage rows + HP damage. Clearing lines pushes the timer back. Yuki can freeze it.
Relics
After each kill, draft 1 of 3 cursed treasures. They stack and radically change your stacking strategy.
Keys
←→ move · ↓ soft drop · ↑/X rotate CW · Z rotate CCW · Space hard drop · C hold · E skill · P pause · M mute.
Heroines
🌸 Aiko (Idol): gold + skill wipes 2 rows.
⚔️ Rin (Ronin): Tetris hits harder + 6-dmg slash.
❄ Yuki (Mage): slower gravity + freezes time.
